# SRW3 Phase 1S — Transition Coverage (spec/transition-coverage.md)

Status: the coverage obligations and their dispositions of record for
mission §5. The battery: `phase1s/proofs/n_s_coverage.k` (generated by
`phase1s/scripts/make_coverage_1s.py`); the mutant controls:
`phase1s/tests/` (generated by `scripts/make_mutant_1s.py`); the static
partition table: `audit/coverage-table-1s.csv`.

## §1 What must be covered (the enumeration of record)

The declared command constructors (the frozen 1P syntax, shared by both
models verbatim):

| constructor | ground shapes |
|---|---|
| `aInitJ(AOk)` | 2 (`aOkT`, `aOkF`) |
| `aExecJ(AOk, ASrc)` | 4 (`2 × 2`) |
| `aGateJ(AOk)` | 2 |
| `aCommitJ(AOk, AOk)` | 4 |

Total **12 ground command shapes**. The coherent state branches of the
constructor-count model: tag-0 (`absStQ(0,false,false,…,D)`) and tag-1
(`absStQ(1,true,true,G,E,…,D)`) with the pool-head fork
(`zeroP | succP(E0)` per pool where the step discriminates). The generator
enumerates the Cartesian table (command shape × branch) from the FROZEN
machine source (pure reader) and emits one ground claim per EXECUTABLE
cell — the partition of record is therefore derived from the same source
file that defines the semantics, and the roundtrip check pins it.

## §3 The mirror partition (`absStepE`) and the cross-model agreement

The integer mirror's rows are the 1P requires-split rows. The generator
emits, for each class, the MIRROR cell claim AND — where the Q side
discriminates constructors while the mirror discriminates Int guards —
the cross-model agreement claim in the image form
(`eraseQ(absStepQ(SQ,c)) ==K absStepE(eraseQ(SQ),c)`), which is the §4
correspondence battery's content at ground granularity:

* empty cell: Q row A-Z (`zeroP`) ↔ mirror row A (`E' <=Int 0`,
  `E' = countToInt(zeroP) = 0` fires by evaluation);
* mint cells: constructor wrap ↔ `+Int 1` (exact, C1.3);
* accept cell: constructor strip ×2 ↔ `-Int 1` ×2 under the wrapped
  requires (exact, C1.4);
* identity/diag cells: identical on both sides.

## §4 No unintended executable transitions

* **The stall discipline:** NO tag-0 exec rule exists on the Q runner (the
  1Q discipline, kept by QS); the STEP-level stall rows (QS ER-1) make the
  tag-0 exec step the IDENTITY. The coverage battery pins the identity;
  the domain discipline (`absExecutableQ(consJ(exec,·), tag-0) = false`)
  is re-anchored (`1S-COV-DOMAIN-PREEXEC`).
* **The `[owise]` complements:** (i) the Q step's complement fires only
  for incoherent tags — pinned by the four representative inertness
  claims (cell 18); (ii) the mirror's complement requires
  `branchTag(C,T) >=Int 2 ∨ <=Int -1`, which for COHERENT bits cannot
  hold (`branchTag(true,true)=1`, `branchTag(false,false)=0` — pinned by
  evaluation) and for MIXED bits is stuck (no rule) — the mixed-bits
  stuckness is witnessed by a MUST-FAIL ground claim (`1S-NEG-MIXED-BITS`,
  `tests/n_s_neg.k`), never asserted positively; (iii) the runner's
  `[owise]` complement is inert on the coherent fragment — inherited from
  the 1Q COH battery and re-anchored by the ground battery's exhaustive
  cells.
* **No unjustified overlap:** the Q rows are constructor-split (free
  constructors: `zeroP` vs `succP` — disjoint by construction, pinned by
  the fork cover G5) and the mirror's requires-pairs are mutually
  exclusive — pinned by the exclusivity claims (`1S-COV-EXCL-GATE`:
  `E' <=Int 0 ∧ E' >=Int 1` is false; `1S-COV-EXCL-COMMIT`:
  `G' >=Int 1 ∧ E' >=Int 1 ∧ (G' <=Int 0 ∨ E' <=Int 0)` is false) and by
  the joint-exhaustiveness witness: for a coherent state and a ground
  command, EXACTLY ONE documented post-state is reachable — the ground
  battery's cells ARE that witness (each cell's claim is deterministic
  and closes).

## §5 The mutant control (the omission detector)

`scripts/make_mutant_1s.py` generates `tests/abs-machine-mut-omit-gatemint-1s.k`:
the QS machine with EXACTLY ONE disclosed row omitted (the gate-mint row
B-S). The coverage battery's cell-10 claim is re-run against the mutant
definition (`1S-NEG-OMIT-GATEMINT`): the step becomes STUCK on that cell
(the incoherent-tag complement cannot fire at tag 1), so the ground claim
**fails/times out** — the omission is DETECTED by the battery. The mutant
is generated, labeled, and confined to `tests/`; it is never imported by
any of-record battery. Result recorded as the negative control it is.

## §6 Mechanization status and the TCB boundary (mission §5's discipline)

* **Mechanized (K, this phase):** every cell of §2/§3 as a ground
  evaluation claim (the partition is DEFINED and CLOSED cell-by-cell);
  the exclusivity pairs; the domain-discipline anchors; the complement
  inertness cells; the mutant detection.
* **Trusted computing base (disclosed, TCB-2):** the ENUMERATION itself —
  that the generated battery's cell list is exactly the declared-rule
  list — is established by the generator reading the frozen source (a
  Python-side structural check, fail-closed, roundtrip-verified) and by
  the retained 1R structural witness (`verify_grid_1r.py`, sha-anchored).
  A green generator run is NOT a mechanized theorem: it is an external
  check with its source pinned. What a reviewer must trust beyond the K
  proofs: (i) the generator faithfully reads the frozen rule heads;
  (ii) the roundtrip (regenerating the battery reproduces it
  byte-identically). Everything downstream of the battery (each cell's
  post-state equality, exclusivity, inertness) IS a K-proved claim.
* **Single deterministic source:** the machine of record is the generated
  QS file; the battery is generated FROM that file; the mutant is
  generated FROM the same source with one disclosed omission. Machine,
  battery, and mutant therefore share one source of truth; the roundtrip
  checks (byte-identical regeneration) are part of the runner's gate.
