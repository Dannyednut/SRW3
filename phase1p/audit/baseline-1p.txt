## 5. Disclosed r2 generator deviations from a naive r1 transcription
(a) the tagged field is named tg (tag is a reserved token in K v7.1.337's
    outer parser — kompile rejects `tag:Int` in a syntax production);
(b) the syntax-production commas are at continuation-line starts (the 1N
    layout); the first template draft omitted them (kompile-fail-closed);
(c) the refinement (eraseT/tagStE/branchTag) is stated over the absStE
    MIRROR sort: SRW3N-ABS cannot be imported into a tagged module without
    importing the 1N runner rules, which would contaminate the tagged
    machine's reachability (the same duplicate-syntax rule-conflict class
    the r1 mutant note records).  The mirror's field correspondence to the
    1N rule table is generator-verified (make_abs_t.py N1_ROWS check).
(d) simJ/absStructOf are deliberately not carried (edit EH, disclosed).
