## 4. Engine API (execution-apis, engine_newPayloadV1+)
   Provides: the CONSENSUS/EXECUTION boundary. The consensus client
   drives payload validation/execution; responses carry {status,
   latestValidHash}. The root becomes AUTHORITATIVE through the
