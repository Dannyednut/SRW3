**Scope.** The cross-phase index of unresolved claims and registered
follow-ups, carried forward from the Phase 1V register (Workstream C of the
1W mandate: "Carry forward the complete Phase 1V register R1–R12. Do not
silently drop, merge, or downgrade unresolved items"). The per-phase ledgers
(`phase1s/claims/claims.csv`, `phase1t/claims/claims-1t.csv`,
`phase1u/claims/claims-1u.csv`, `phase1v/claims/claims-1v.csv`,
`phase1w/claims/claims-1w.csv`) remain APPEND-ONLY and authoritative for
their own evidence; this register only INDEXES what is still open and maps
each item to its root-cause class, attempted methods, and current status.

are excluded.
