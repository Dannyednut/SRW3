follow-ups, carried forward from the Phase 1W register (mandate: carry
R1–R14 with NO silent drops, merges, or downgrades; R13 may be closed ONLY
if all eight of its closure conditions are satisfied by executed evidence).

`phase1x/claims/claims-1x.csv`) remain APPEND-ONLY and authoritative for
their own evidence; this register only INDEXES what is still open and maps
each item to its root-cause class, attempted methods, and current status.

are excluded.
